Melodys Support

Import your music

Tap + in Library and choose audio files from Files. To bring artwork and lyrics along, import a Melodys package or select the matching .mi file and its original audio. Melodys matches details to the audio content, including after a rename.

Try a sample library

Download four original instrumental samples with their artwork. Save the ZIP in Files and import it in Melodys.

Transfer between devices

Open Transfer on the receiving phone. Enable receiving and approve the connection. For browser uploads, keep both devices on the same Wi-Fi and enter the address shown on the phone.

Choose the display language

Open Settings → Language. Select System Default, 简体中文, or English. Your original track titles and lyrics stay in their original language.

Contact support

Email lm@usev.cn with your app version and the steps where you need help.

Melodys 技术支持

导入音乐

点曲库右上角的加号，从「文件」选择音频。如果要一起导入封面和歌词，可选取音乐包，或同时选取原始音频和对应的 .mi 资料文件。音频改名后仍可按内容匹配资料。

试用示例曲库

下载四首原创演示器乐及配套封面。将 ZIP 保存到「文件」，再从曲库导入。

在设备之间传歌

在接收设备打开「传输」，开启接收并确认连接。电脑上传时，将两台设备接入同一 Wi-Fi，在浏览器输入手机显示的地址。

更换界面语言

打开「设置 → 界面语言」，选择跟随系统、简体中文或 English。歌曲标题与歌词保留原文。

联系技术支持

请将应用版本和遇到问题的操作步骤发到 lm@usev.cn。
